## 6. Rights
### What is and is not public domain
| Layer | Australian rule | Our case | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Composition (melody) | Creator died before 1955: public domain | Twinkle tune 1761, Beethoven d. 1827: free | [1][2] |
| Words | Same rule as music | Jane Taylor d. 1824: free; modern translations of Ode: check | [1][15] |
| New arrangement | Can carry its own copyright in the new material; the arranger counts in the term | Do not copy any modern arrangement, harmony or backing | [3]; secondary sources, snippet |
| Published edition (layout of a printed score) | 25 years from first publication | Do not scan or copy a modern edition; typing the bare melody line from it is not copying the layout | [2][3] |
| Sound recording | Generally 70 years from the end of the year of first release [19]; recordings made before 1 Jan 1955 expired [1]; unreleased recordings follow other rules | Never use anyone's recording, including YouTube karaoke (R13) | [1][19] |
| Performers | Consent needed to record a live performance; performers co-own the recording with its maker | Applies to any recording the team makes | [4][19] |
- One item can hold several copyrights; all must be clear before copying [2].
- IMSLP labels follow its own server rules; for Australia check the 25-year edition rule yourself [8].

### What synthesising in-app from our own note list avoids
- No sound-recording copyright (nothing recorded), no performer consent, no arrangement or edition copied.
- Key and tempo changes are free (build-plan section 6): no re-licensing per key.
- Still owed: the synth sound's own licence (Tone.js oscillator = no samples; Salamander piano = credit, CC BY) per tech-keyboard-input.md section 9.

### What a team-made recording requires
- Usually the maker ("the person who operates the recording equipment", Arts Law's words) and the performers co-own the recording; per the same sheet, a person who pays for the recording owns it, though employment and written agreements can change this; a free studio recording belongs to the studio unless agreed in writing [19].
  o Plain-language sheet, not legal advice; for a real release, check the Act or the Copyright Council.
- Copyright can only be transferred by a signed written document [19].
- So: a one-paragraph signed consent from every performer (use in app, pitch, any IP transfer the challenge rules set).
- A non-team singer or player: same consent plus a written licence or assignment; never a minor without a guardian `unverified` (privacy-design-v0.md owns this).
- Simpler: do not record a backing at all; the synth guide plus drone already meets R13.

## 7. Pitfalls when the phrase is a live pitch-detection target
| Pitfall | What happens | One mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Octave error | Tracker names the right note an octave off; zero in the synthetic test, real rooms unknown [tech-pitch-detection.md M1] | Set the expected octave from the range check; a note exactly 12 semitones off gets its own "octave" flag (low clarity = tracker, high clarity = learner), never silently scored right |
| Vibrato and wobble | Reading follows the wave; typical vibrato about 5 to 7 Hz [20] (snippet) | Median over 100 to 200 ms per note before scoring cents (tech-pitch-detection.md M1) |
| Consonants and breath | "Tw", "k", "st" give no pitch and fire energy onsets early | Score takes on "loo"; use the pitch onset, not the energy onset, for timing (tech-pitch-detection.md section 3) |
| Hall noise and applause | Frames below the clarity gate drop out; M1 cue fires | Keyboard demo runs on USB MIDI (exact); voice only as replay or in a quiet side room |
| Phone mic processing | Echo cancelling, noise suppression and auto gain bend pitch and level | Request all three off in getUserMedia (tech-pitch-detection.md section 4), then read track.getSettings() to confirm the device obeyed |
| Stage preflight | Mic permission, MIDI device, click-to-input delay fail silently | One preflight screen: mic on localhost or HTTPS, MIDI device listed, tap-along delay check (M2); rehearse the build-plan fallback ladder |
| Guide-tone bleed | The app hears its own guide melody and scores it | Guide in headphones, or guide only in the count-in bar, drone off while scoring |
| Harder phrase top note | A flat A4 may be the learner or the tracker on a weak voice | Show "close to A" bands (50-cent line, tech-feedback-layer.md), never a red cross at 10 cents |
