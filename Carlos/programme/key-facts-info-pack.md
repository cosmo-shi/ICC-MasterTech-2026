- Daily rhythm: "teams attend a mandatory meeting at the start and end of each day" whether on-site or off-site (p7).
- A Webex "window" is "open all day, every day" (p7).
- Prep days are described as "Mandatory preparatory activities", with attendance "crucial" (p3).
- The pack states the program runs "2 to 12 November 2026" (p3). Residential part runs Mon 9 to Thu 12 Nov (p3).
- Setup before 2 Nov: install Webex and MS Teams, both spaces named "MasterTech 2026" (p7).
- Sponsor announcement: the 2026 industry sponsor will be announced at the 2 Nov meeting (p3, p4).
- Awards: held at the close of Wed 11 Nov. No awards time is given (p7).

## 2. Deliverable format

- Required team output: "a functioning technology prototype, a business case and a stage-ready pitch" (p5).
- Final stage: "serious pitch rehearsal, a live stage presentation and an expo-style showcase" (p4).
- Each team must "explain the problem, the working solution and what should happen next" (p7).
- Showcase style: "in the style of a commercial expo or trade show" (p7).
- Showcase venue: NIIN stand in the World of Solutions, each team has a workspace there (p4, p7).
- Audience: "thousands of in-person Cisco Live attendees and a much larger online audience" (p4).
- Teams "document their journey" during the sprint (p4). No format for this is given.
- Who presents: not stated. Team size is three people per team (p3).
- Pitch length: NOT STATED in the pack. Carlos confirmed 5 Nov: 3 minutes.
- Slides or demo rules: NOT STATED beyond "working demonstrations" and "functioning technology prototype" (p3, p5).
- Submission deadline and method: NOT STATED. Pack says only "Teams face fixed deadlines" (p5).
- Time limit hint: teams deliver a final demonstration "within the time limits of the competition" (p4). Limits not given.
- Pace expectation: "Come ready to test ideas, ask for help, build working demonstrations and rehearse a clear pitch for a live audience." (p3)
- Team structure: seven teams, one per hub: Melbourne, Perth, Canberra, Adelaide, Brisbane, Sydney, Singapore (p3).

## 3. Judging

- No scoring rubric, weighting or named judges appear in the pack.
- Success criteria: "Challenge success criteria introduced to each team" on Mon 9 Nov at 8am at ICM (p6). Teams meet at Innovation Central Melbourne in the morning where "the challenge success criteria are introduced" (p7).
- Review criteria, exact wording: "Reviewers test customer value, evidence, technical feasibility and what it would take to proceed." (p5)
- Standard, exact wording: "Real problem. Working prototype. Technology prototype. Business case. Live scrutiny. Pitching to win." (p5, "THE STANDARD" graphic)
- Who judges, exact wording: "ICM, NIIN and Cisco convene experienced reviewers." (p5)
- Sponsor role, exact wording: "to sharpen the challenge and test assumptions, not prescribe the answer." Sponsor gives "direct feedback" (p5).
- Context test, exact wording: "Teams must understand the operating context, users and constraints, then show how a credible idea can create value." (p5)
- Elimination: "Teams are not eliminated, but often the details of their ideas are." (p7)
- Prize details: none stated. Only "The day closes with awards." (p7)

## 5. Logistics

- Cisco Live registration: "Your Cisco Live registration details will be sent to you by email before the event." (p2) Registration also happens on arrival at WoS on Mon 9 Nov (p6, p7).
- Accommodation: La Trobe University provides Menzies College, Bundoora campus, Sun 8 to Thu 12 Nov 2026 (p3, p6).
- Accommodation details (check-in and out, parking, FAQs) are in the Pre-Arrival Pack, not in this PDF (p2, p7).
- Meals: breakfast and dinner provided (p3). Welcome dinner Sun 8 Nov at 6pm. Closing BBQ Thu 12 Nov, 11:30am to 1pm at WoS (p6).
- Lunch: on arrival at WoS on Mon 9 Nov (p6). Other lunches not stated. Breakfast at WoS on Wed 11 Nov (p6).
- Transport in Melbourne: buses between Bundoora and MCEC during the event. Times to be confirmed in the Pre-Arrival Pack (p3, p6).
- Own transport: "Participants are responsible for their own transport to and from the airport, and outside the dates above." (p3)
- Venue: Melbourne Convention and Exhibition Centre (MCEC), World of Solutions, NIIN stand (p3, p4).
- Teams workspace: each team has a workspace at the NIIN stand in WoS (p7).
- Digital tools: Webex is "the live communication channel". Teams is "the working file space". Keep both open on hybrid and residential days (p8).
- Local contact: "Your Innovation Central Director remains your first point of contact for local travel, team coordination and university-specific questions" (p8).
- What to bring: not stated. Dress code: not stated. Dietary needs are collected in the arrival survey (p2).
