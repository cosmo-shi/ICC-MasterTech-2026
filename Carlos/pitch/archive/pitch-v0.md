## 2. Spoken script with second counts
### Beat 1 - Carlos (0:00-0:26, 64 words)
- "This year I started recording vocal covers at home, alone. A vocal coach gave me anxiety, so I asked an AI."
- "Everything I typed about my voice pointed the wrong way, because it could not hear me."
- "Then one cue: don't get louder, narrow the vowel. One retake, measurably better."
- "The note I still could not reach needed a teacher. Nobody sent me."
  o Source: talking-script Picture 0; measured change = research-bank.md, first-hand evidence table, row 2 (n = 1).

### Beat 2 - Simone (0:26-0:44, 41 words)
- "Three of us, one gap: nobody heard us and told us one thing to fix."
- "Now picture a Year 5 girl, three hours from Canberra, in a small school."
- "Primary teaching degrees average about eight hours of compulsory music, in total."
  o Statistic: research-bank.md, music-education table, row "Pre-service hours" (7.98 average, Fading Notes 2023).
  o Picture A is a composite (talking-script); say "picture", never "meet".

### Beat 3 - Justin (0:44-1:00, 40 words)
- "So we built a coach that hears how you play. You name a goal: play this song for Mum."
- "Each small goal carries one fundamental. Ten seconds, one fix, a retake that shows it."
- "Two lines: your goal, your skill. Watch."
  o If demo variant A (on-screen keys) is live, say "a coach that follows how you play"; never claim listening that is not live.

### Beat 4 - Justin, live demo (1:00-2:00, 56 words)
- Full run in section 4; spoken lines only here:
  o 1:00 "My goal is the song. Today's small goal: the first phrase, steady."
  o 1:12 (plays; no words)
  o 1:20 "One thing, in plain words, and it shows me what it wanted."
  o 1:30 (retake; no words)
  o 1:42 "The retake shows it worked. My goal moved, and my skill moved."
  o 1:50 "And when the next step needs a real teacher, it offers me one, with my recording and my fix list."

### Beat 5 - Carlos (2:00-2:15, 36 words)
- "Our proposed buyer is universities: UC already runs free outreach into small rural schools, with no music we could find."
- "Videos cannot hear you. This can, and it hands you to a human. We lived that gap."
  o Variant A live: say "Videos cannot see how you play. This can." (same length).
  o Source: verification-2026-11-06-afternoon.md section 3 (Possible Future Passport, UC 4 Yourself; "none confirmed" for music); research-bank.md, meeting-2 table, row "UC does outreach".

### Beat 6 - Carlos (2:15-2:24, 23 words)

  o Source: universities-brief.md section 4, ethics timing (UC low risk "usually assessed within two weeks", opened); applies if pilot data count as research.

### Beat 7 - Carlos (2:24-2:27, 7 words)
- "Next: a buddy, shared instruments, more instruments."

### Beat 8 - Carlos or Simone (2:27-2:40, 27 words)

  o Slow down; pause after "postcode"; no slide change during the line.

## 4. Live demo, beat by beat (60 seconds), and fallback
- Instrument: keyboard (team lean; decisions-log.md 6 Nov, "feedback works on a tablet without extra hardware"). Voice stays as the story.
- Phrase: one rights-cleared or public-domain tune, first phrase only (requirement R13; no YouTube karaoke on stage).
- Input, two variants; pick by Tuesday 15:00 feature freeze:
  o Variant A (default): piano layout on the tablet screen; robust, and proves the "no instrument needed" path (Kat's point).
  o Variant B: real keyboard, phone microphone listening; proves "it hears you"; only if the live line is reliable in rehearsal.
