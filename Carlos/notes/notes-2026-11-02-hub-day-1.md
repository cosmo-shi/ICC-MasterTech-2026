## Scott's example use cases (his own projects, for calibration)
- Learning journey assistant: past subject scores, find gaps, generate quizzes to backfill before next semester.
- Internship matching by semantic analysis of CVs, replacing about 500 admin hours per 100 students.
- Learn to code by reverse-engineering top open-source projects with AI explaining them.
- Problem he sees: students feed the rubric to AI and ask for a "defensible solution".
