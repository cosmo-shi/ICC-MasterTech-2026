## 2. The idea as it stands (one key thing)
- A practice coach for music fundamentals on a phone, with or without an instrument. It listens, says one thing to fix, and the retake shows it worked. It never generates music.
- The two-track goal (Carlos, 6 Nov): the learner names the result they want ("make this cover sound good", "play this song for mum"); the AI splits it into small goals; each small goal carries one fundamental; two progress lines, "your goal" and "your skill".
  o So the learner improves on the thing they want and improves overall, without being told they are doing a course. Gamification = structure and habit, not a game.
- The ceiling: when a fix needs real technique, the app hands the learner to a human teacher with the recording and the fix list. Augment, never replace.
- Learner: a young person without access (regional, rural, remote, low income); adult beginners welcome. Stakeholder: universities as a sector, through outreach and pathway programmes (not only enrolled students).
- Secondary, on a vision slide: community layer (shared instruments, a buddy, level-matched jam), more instruments, learning styles.
- The hook (Stanley, Chris): "videos cannot see or hear how you play; this can", plus one fix with proof, plus the hand-off.

## 5. What the research established (say this, not that)

| Sponsor universities | None has a music school; Flinders' music-industry degree is in teach-out from 2026; La Trobe closed music in 1999; music sits in one arts unit each (UC 9885, Flinders EDUC2338, La Trobe EDU3009) | universities-brief.md |

## 12. Risks
- Prior art: AI music coaches exist; our claim is the combination, never "first".
- The "trick" doubt: hidden fundamentals may feel like homework; only a learner test answers it.
- Scope: three minutes; one instrument, one stage, one buyer; everything else is the last slide.
- Feasibility: live pitch tracking on a phone, offline; prove the loop on one phrase before anything else.
- Repo sync: the folder is a Drive mirror; commit from one machine only and wait for sync before opening on the other.
