# Carlos's personal hackathon notes (pasted 5 Nov 2026, lightly tidied)

## Challenge and sponsors
- "How might we harness AI and emerging technologies to transform the way students learn and succeed?"
- Sponsors: La Trobe University, Cisco, University of Canberra, Flinders University.

## Problem as Carlos sees it
- Students lean on AI to do all the schoolwork.
- Professors cannot tell if work is AI, nor how much AI use is acceptable.
  o Even where AI is allowed, "how much" is rarely defined.

## Idea
- App with safeguards and harnesses; students use any frontier model within university policy.
  o Every model knows it serves a student and must act with integrity.
  o Prompts are monitored and graded: tokens used, prompting efficiency, quality of questioning.

## Hackathon lessons (past Australian hackathons)
- Best answer integrates the sponsor company's technology into the solution.
  o Priority is the company's motive and convenience, not showing off skill or the newest tech.
- Be realistic, creative and simple. Take shortcuts; it is a demo.
- Australian audiences: scared to be first to use new tech; care about privacy.
  o They do not want to give access to their phone or equipment.
- Slides: Slidesgo templates.

## Dad's response
- True critical, analytical and first-principles thinking shows in how a user prompts AI.
  o An app that grades or reviews students on those three things is a big step.
- Integrate AI-assisted learning with Outcome Based Education (OBE) principles.
  o OBE-based grading is the key.
