## Pitch skeleton (fill from problem-framing.md and solution-directions.md)
### 1. The problem
- One student in one moment: 11 pm, case study due, AI open, unsure what is allowed, stuck, no one to ask.
- Why it matters: cognitive offloading, unclear AI rules, 450 students per coordinator. One number each, from the research bank.
- Why now: AI use is universal, policy is unclear, and nobody can guide 450 students one to one.

### 2. The working solution
- One sentence on what it is. Then the live demo, about 60 seconds, student's hands on the keyboard.
  o Beat 1: student opens the brief, sees the "how you may use AI here" card.
  o Beat 2: asks "write my answer"; the guide asks back; student reasons to the next step.
  o Beat 3: weekly check lights a stuck outcome on their map; journey record shows progress.
- Guardrails and privacy: assignment-scoped, opt-in, data stays with the university, no device access.
- Where Cisco technology fits, stated plainly, no overclaim.
- Evidence: one user test, one quote from an expert session, one measured result.

### 3. What should happen next
- By-products of the same data: anonymous cohort heatmap for the coordinator, opt-in journey view for markers.
- Pilot in one unit next semester as an optional study aid: who owns it, what it costs, what we measure.
- Three risks and the mitigation for each: privacy, adoption, wrong generated rules.
- The ask: a sponsor contact, a unit to pilot with, access to approved tools.
