### 1.3b Night rules added from the check (not in v1; team may strike)
- Cue cutoff: if no cue shows 30 s after take end, F says only "Please wait a little longer"; at 60 s log a cue failure (counts as a miss for C2), F says "We will move on," and the session continues with the next take. Never repair live.
- Optional extra phrase ("yes" to another phrase): cap at 2 minutes; if the slot is at 20 minutes, skip it and log "offered, not taken (time)".
- Stopped or withdrawn session: delete any recording in front of the person, tick `audio_deleted_yes`, write only "stopped" or "withdrawn" and the minute; no other field is filled.
- Free-text rule: L writes comments with any name, place, job, course or other detail that points to a person removed or replaced by "[removed]".
- The consent and invitation say "one phrase, a few times"; groups B and C make three recorded takes. That matches the card ("a few times").

### 1.4 Waiting time and must-not-say
- Equal on-screen pause: A sees its cue about when B and C see "Ready for take 2" or "Try again."; L notes any slip.
- No praise or blame: not "good", "nice", "better", "that was off", "you improved".
- No technique words unless the screen shows them: "breath", "support", "pitch", "flat", "sharp", "wobble", "steady", "vowel".
- No explaining, repeating or demonstrating the cue; no humming the note again; no hint of what the groups are.
- If asked "is it AI?": "The suggestion comes from rules on the phone, and from a language model when it is online; today it is offline."
- If asked for help: "I can't help during the test; do whatever feels right." Never fill silences with reassurance; never push a rating.
- Only the built-in phrase; no songs the person might know. No photo or video of the screen with a face in it.

### 1.5 The three rating questions (read exactly; the person circles a 1 to 5 card)
- "How much do you agree: after that screen, I know what to do next? One is not at all, five is completely." (`know_next`)
- "How much do you trust that suggestion for your next try? One is not at all, five is completely." (`trust`)
- "How much do you agree: the suggestion was understandable and worth trying? One is not at all, five is completely." (`useful`)

## 2. Consent script (read aloud, verbal yes, no signature)

> "We are students testing an unfinished music app for the MasterTech Challenge. It takes 20 minutes: you sing one short phrase a few times and answer questions. You may see slightly different screens from other people. Singing can feel awkward; skip anything or stop any time, with no reason needed. The phone stays offline and your voice is processed on this phone only. I delete the audio in front of you. ## 3. Scorer sheet (print one per scorer; scorers work apart and blind)

- Blind to group, take order and cue text. Traces arrive as random codes in one folder, scored in one pass.
- The count itself comes from the frozen script. Scorers do not count by hand; they run the same script on the same files, then independently judge the flags below.
- Rule as v1 defines it (do not edit; a change after evening one is reported as a change):
  o Window: 1.5 s fixed, starting 250 ms after the detected held-note onset. Never cut by the detected end. If the take ends before the window closes: unscorable, never "fewer".
  o Unvoiced: clarity below 0.9. No interpolation; a gap ends any excursion. Unscorable if under 70% of window frames are voiced or any gap exceeds 200 ms.
  o Octave frames: any frame 900 cents or more from the reference is dropped as unvoiced. Over 10% of window frames dropped: unscorable. Never fixed by hand.
  o Reference: median of the voiced window frames of that take, not the target note.
  o Excursion: unbroken run of same-sign frames more than 50 cents from the reference, lasting 100 ms or more. Ends on 100 ms back in band, or a sign flip. One long excursion counts once; runs under 100 ms count zero.
  o Order of filters: drop unvoiced, drop octave frames, median, then excursions.
- Worked check: reference line 0, in band to 1.0 s, 70 cents high for 0.3 s, back in band 0.2 s, then 60 cents low for 0.15 s = 2 excursions. A 90 ms run counts zero.
