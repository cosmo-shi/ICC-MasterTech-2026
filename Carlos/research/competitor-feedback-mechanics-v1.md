## 6. Saturday 10-minute hands-on script (phone, two apps, 5 minutes each)
- Setup (before the clock): fresh install or fresh account if free; note phone model, OS, date, app version in the record; screen recording on.
  o Record in a quiet room; use the free tier only; do not enter payment details; stop at any paywall and write "paywall at step N".
### 6a. June (guitar-led; if no guitar, test the chat and goal flow, sing or hum a note, and mark "instrument not used")
- Minutes 0 to 2: (budget 20 minutes plus setup in total: 10 per app) open, tell June a goal in one sentence; screenshot the plan it returns.
  o Look for: does the plan name a result or only a topic?
- Minutes 2 to 6: play or sing one phrase twice, once wrong on purpose (flat note, late beat).
  o Look for what June says after each attempt; screenshot each message.
- Minutes 6 to 8: ask "why was that wrong?" and "what one thing should I change?".
  o Look for the answer length and whether it names one change.
- Minutes 8 to 10: repeat the phrase once after fixing it; screenshot any comparison or praise.
  o Look for a side-by-side, a score change or a "better than last time" line.
### 6b. Yousician (singing lesson on the free tier)
- Same four steps; add: open Record and Review after the take and play it back.
  o Look for any goal screen at onboarding and any summary after a lesson.
### 6c. Scoring rubric (one line per app, copy into the notes)
| Test | Counts as a yes when | Does not count |
|---|---|---|
| States a cause | A message names why the take missed in plain words (e.g. "you started the note too low and rose into it") tied to your take | "Try again", "off pitch", a red mark, a score, a generic tip not about your take |
| Gives one fix | The app names one concrete change for the next take (one sentence, one action) | A list of three or more tips, "do this exercise" with no link to your mistake |
| Shows a before and after | The app places your first take and your retake side by side or states the change (e.g. "pitch error down from X to Y") | Streaks, XP, "good job", playing back one take without comparison |
- Use one fixed phrase for both apps, record a baseline take, change one thing, then retake the same phrase. A retake is the second take after the app has spoken.
  o P means one of the three is present or the app shows part of it; a feature the free tier hides is "?" (unavailable), never N.
  o Also note: does the app refer to a teacher, and does it still give feedback in flight mode (2 extra minutes, optional).
- Record per app: Y, P or N for each of the three, the screenshot filenames, and the time to first feedback in seconds.
- Stage line after the test: if all N or P, say "In our hands-on test of June and Yousician on 7 November, neither named a cause, gave one fix and showed a before-and-after on the retake"; if any is Y, say what it did instead.
  o Never say it unless the notes show all three rows for both apps.
