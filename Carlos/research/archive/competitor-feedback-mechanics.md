# Competitor feedback mechanics: what the music apps hear, say and miss

- Date: Fri 6 Nov 2026 (AEDT), overnight draft; prices are US App Store USD seen 6 Nov 2026.
- Purpose: the app survey an advisor asked for: "survey the music apps, state what is missing, and where the AI feedback comes in".
- Scope: 21 products, one level below trend-check-music-learning.md (35 products, prices, verdicts); feedback mechanics only.
- Status: overnight draft for the team; verify before the pitch. Codex critique folded in; in-app checks still owed (Open questions).

## 1. Conclusion first
- Listening is common ground: 19 of 21 products hear the learner (mic, MIDI or camera) and most mark notes during the take.
  o Do not pitch "it listens" as the difference; Yousician, Simply, flowkey, Skoove, Riyaz, Sing Sharp all do.
- In the 21 products and pages reviewed, we did not verify any app that combines a plain-words cause, one fix, and a shown before-and-after on the retake.
  o Nearest: June (goal-led chat, "a tailored next step"), ROLI's AI coach (spoken technique cues, open beta, camera hardware), Sing Sharp (resonance view plus AI mentor).
  o Many cells are "not stated" rather than "no": an in-app check of June and Yousician is owed before stage.
- Human links exist mostly for a teacher the learner already has: Yousician Edu, flowkey for Teachers, Piano Marvel, SmartMusic.
  o Human video feedback exists as a paid course service (ArtistWorks Video Exchange); we did not verify an AI coach that refers onward at its ceiling with the take attached.
- "Works without an instrument" is NOT a difference on its own: 11 of 21 do it (every singing app, Duolingo).
- Range check plus key change is shared ground too (Simply Sing).
- Our defensible line: the combination (own goal, one cause-and-fix with proof, hand-off at the ceiling, offline loop), shown working in the demo, not any single feature.

## 4. Common ground and where our combination differs
- Common ground, say it plainly (do not overclaim):
  o Live listening and right-or-wrong marking: Yousician, Simply, flowkey, Skoove, Playground, Riyaz, Sing Sharp, Trala, Chordie.
  o Range check and key change: Simply Sing "finds your voice type, adjusts songs to you"; Sing Sharp tracks range. Our R4 is shared ground.
  o An AI teacher you talk to: June (chat, guitar) and ROLI's coach (spoken, beta, camera hardware).
  o Practice without an instrument: every singing app; Simply Piano touch courses; Duolingo on-screen keyboard.
  o Teacher links exist, for a teacher you already have: Yousician Edu, flowkey for Teachers, Piano Marvel.
- Where our combination differs (as a set, not one feature):
  o Starts from the learner's own result, then splits it into small goals with one fundamental each (only June states goal-led, guitar only).
  o Names the cause in plain words, gives ONE fix, and shows the before and after on the retake; we did not verify all three in any surveyed app.
  o Hands the learner to a human at the technique ceiling, with the recording and the fix list; surveyed tools serve a teacher already in place (class, course or dashboard).
  o Target: the feedback loop runs offline on a low-cost phone; no surveyed page states that; we must show device and latency results.
  o Two progress lines (your goal, your skill); others show one line of levels, stars, XP or streaks.
- For the judge who asks "why is this better than Yousician?":
  o "Yousician already gives live note feedback and practice exercises. Our difference is one explained fix, then the retake shows it worked; here it is on one phrase." (Then demo it.)
  o "Yousician's own AI teacher, June, shows the direction is right; ours starts from your goal, works on a cheap phone offline, and ends at a human teacher."
  o "We are not a bigger song library; we are the feedback step, and we hand off rather than replace."
- Risk to say out loud if asked: June or ROLI could add cause-and-proof quickly; our edge is the set plus the access focus and the hand-off, not a secret algorithm.

## Open questions
- PracticeFirst, Simply Guitar, EarMaster and Vocal Image were suggested but not checked; check before claiming the survey is complete.
- Offline: install the top five (Yousician, Simply Piano, Simply Sing, Riyaz, Sing Sharp) and test in flight mode; 18 of 21 cells are unknown.

- Nine-student test: does "one cause, one fix, proof" beat "right or wrong marks" for a beginner? That is our claim to prove, not to assert.
- AUD prices: confirm on an Australian phone before quoting on stage.
