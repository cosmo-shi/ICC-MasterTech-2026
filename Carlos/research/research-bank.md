## Gaps

- Marker workload for process evidence at scale: no study measures marking time for prompt logs.
  o Nothing found on a 450-student unit; the staff survey above only covers productivity.
- Student views on sharing prompt logs with staff: no Australian survey found.
  o Closest leads were US student-advocacy papers, not verified and not Australian.
- Accuracy of AI-generated process summaries or diagrams: no study found on faithfulness to real logs.
  o The pitch should treat summary accuracy as a pilot question, not a proven fact.
- Government pages (TEQSA, JSA, Department of Education) timed out repeatedly during checks.
  o TEQSA and JSA wording above comes from Future Campus summaries, not the primary text.
  o Australian Framework for Generative AI in Schools: only 2023 coverage seen, 2025 review unverified.
- Peak body positions from Universities Australia and the Group of Eight: not found or verified.
- Guardrailed tutor trials in Australian universities: none found with learning outcomes measured.
  o Bastani is Turkish high school maths; Kestin is one Harvard physics course.
- Deakin and Sydney "Talk is cheap" paper (Corbin, Dawson, Liu 2025): publisher page blocked, abstract unverified.
- Australian employer surveys on graduate AI skills: none verified beyond the JSA summary.
- Institutional AI licences (heard 5 Nov from an advisor, unverified): Flinders gives all students Gemini Enterprise; UNSW gives ChatGPT.
  o Verify on the universities' own pages before use; strong "what it takes to proceed" evidence if true.
