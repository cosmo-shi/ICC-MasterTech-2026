- Date: Fri 6 to Sat 7 Nov 2026 (AEDT), overnight. Purpose: give the team the evidence to settle sheet row A4 on Sunday, or show what public sources cannot settle.
- Scope: Outreach Funding guidance (full PDF opened tonight), ACT school structure and age rule, partner-map year bands, ACT university outreach bands, beginner start ages, consent and safeguarding by age, nearest-product age ratings.
- Status: overnight draft for the team; verify before the pitch. Lean: Year 5 to 6, provisional and testable. Source numbers in [brackets] are in `## Sources`; team files are named in full.

## 1. Bottom line
- The public evidence narrowly favours **Year 5 to 6 (about age 10 to 12)**; Year 7 to 9 is the fallback.
  o This reverses the lean in audience-statement-v0.md and sheet row A4 (Year 7 to 9); see section 2 totals: 12 vs 9.
  o The margin is small and rests on the delivery ecosystem, not on learning evidence; without the "picture already in the pitch" row it is 10 vs 9.
- The three facts that settle it:
  o 1. The Outreach Funding guidance names no school year and no activity medium; it says "target people at different life stages" [1] p4. Funding does not choose the band; the delivery partner does.
  o 2. The ACT and partner-map music programmes start at Year 5: ACT IMP selects Years 5 and 6 (selective, two-year commitment) [3][4]; Music in Me, Song Room, Just Brass are primary; UC's Possible Future Passport is K to 6 rural primary within three hours [5].
  o 3. Year 7 to 9 buys no lighter consent: every on-time Year 7 to 9 student is under 15 at the start of the school year (section 3), so the same guardian regime applies as for Year 5 to 6.
- What public sources cannot settle: whether any outreach team or programme would accept a music practice activity, for which year, as "building aspiration for higher education". No opened page says music or software qualifies; only an outreach manager can say.
- Sentence to say on stage (19 words): "We propose Year 5 and 6 beginners, about ten to twelve; university outreach as payer, university students as mentors."
- What still argues for Year 7 to 9: the fear and self-efficacy studies are adolescent (12 to 19) [team: social-and-fear-evidence-v1.md E1, E3]; UC 4 Yourself is a Year 9 regional programme within three hours [5].

## 4. What the Outreach Funding Program guidance says
- Opened tonight: "Outreach Funding Guidance", 6 pages, footer "Updated 19 February 2026" [1]. It covers "the use of Outreach Funding in 2026" (p1); 2027 rules not located.
- The legislative basis is Part 9B of the Higher Education Support (Other Grants) Guidelines 2022, inserted by F2025L01613 [15]; only its contents list loaded (66AA objectives, 66AB eligibility of bodies corporate, 66AF conditions). The register lists the amending instrument "no longer in force" (snippet), the usual state once amendments are absorbed (`unverified`).

| Topic | Quoted line | Page |
|---|---|---|
| Objective | "build aspiration in target cohorts to enrol in and attend higher education" | p1 |
| Eligible participants | "Funding must only be used to deliver outreach activities (pre access stage) which target the following cohorts": First Nations peoples; low-SES by home address ("lowest quartile of the ... SEIFA Index of Education and Occupation by Statistical Area Level 1"); regional or remote people "who have not yet made a decision to study (pre-access stage)" | p2 |
| Year levels | None named. "Providers should deliver activities which target people at different life stages, including school leavers, mature-age students, mid-career changers, etc." (Principle 4) | p4 |
| Who to reach | "primarily target people who are not already actively planning to engage in university-level study" (Principle 1) | p3 |
| Verification | "reasonable steps to confirm that participants are part of the target cohort ... How this is verified is up to each provider" | p3 |
| Spill-over | "some activities intended to target priority equity cohorts may also incidentally benefit other participants" | p3, p5 |
| Eligible activities | Outreach "build aspiration for higher education, for example by": reducing barriers ("financial assistance"); informing about benefits; informing about pathways and supports "(including careers advice)"; navigating the system; "academic tutoring or guidance" (excluding pathway or preparation courses, funded through Fee-Free Uni Ready) | p3 |
| Ineligible | "General marketing and recruitment activities"; "Support services which are targeted to students already enrolled in higher education"; delivery of FFUR courses; "Activities which target people who are not part of a defined target cohort" | p5 |
| Software, tools, music | Not mentioned anywhere in the 6 pages. The only material line: consider whether outreach materials need "specific institutional branding, or whether provider agnostic approaches may be more appropriate" | p4 |
| Barriers named | Societal ("lack of available role models", "age barriers"), financial, information, geographic ("availability of study options locally") | p4 |
| Collaboration and evidence | Partner with VET, community organisations, industry (Principle 7); activities "based on best-practice and evidence of 'what works'" (Principle 8) | p4, p5 |
| Evaluation | "Providers must have clear arrangements in place to evaluate funded outreach activities"; use SEHEEF | p5 |
| Reporting | Financial Acquittal and Performance Report "by 27 March 2027"; per activity: description, target cohort, aim and success measure, outcomes, "number of participants engaged"; certification by the Vice Chancellor or delegate | p5, p6 |
| Rollover | "limited opportunity to rollover any remaining funding into 2027", in writing, at the Department's discretion | p2 |
| Contact | equityprograms@education.gov.au | p6 |

- What this changes:
  o The guidance does not exclude a music activity, and does not include one; a music practice coach fits only as the vehicle of an aspiration activity (role models, pathway information), as business-case-v1.md s1 already says.
  o A software or licence cost is neither allowed nor barred by the text; whether it is a fundable activity cost is for the provider's own rules (`unverified`).
  o The band choice is free under the guidance; the cohort test (home address, identity, regional) is the binding filter.
