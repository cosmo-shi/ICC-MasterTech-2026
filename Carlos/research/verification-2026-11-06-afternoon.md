## 1

- Timeline (Wikipedia, opened, secondary): founded 1965; joined the Canberra School of Art in 1988 to form the Canberra Institute of the Arts. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/ANU_School_of_Music
  o ANU archive (opened): the Institute "amalgamated with the The Australian National University in January 1992". https://archivescollection.anu.edu.au/index.php/institute-of-arts
- ANU history page (snippet; fetch blocked): the Minister's amalgamation plan increased "the School's focus on research". No 1992 cost motive found.
  o https://music.cass.anu.edu.au/about-us/history-school-music . No source for a formal rule that academics must publish.
- 2012, CityNews 15 Jun 2012 (opened): academic staff "from 24 full time equivalent ... down to 13"; admin "10 ... to eight".
  o Deficit "$2.9 million a year" against a "$1.4 million" subsidy. Students keep "a minimum of one hour a week of one-to-one tuition". https://citynews.com.au/2012/music-school-plan-to-go-ahead/
- Earlier plan, Green Left 11 May 2012 (opened): "up to 32 job cuts"; one-on-one tuition "will no longer be a part of the course". Offered "a small allowance of $600 a semester" instead; the plan then softened by June. https://greenleft.org.au/node/53469
- Review (author: Andrew Podger, 2016): Wikipedia (opened) says it called for "an overhaul of its governance and financial management".
  o Snippets only: "not delivering the excellence in teaching required of a top university"; acclaimed musicians left within 18 months.
- 2025: ANU says the change proposal was issued 3 July 2025. Canberra Times 16 Sep 2025 (opened): cuts would "effectively axe the school altogether". https://www.canberratimes.com.au/story/9066854/music-students-leave-university-amid-proposed-renew-anu-cuts/
- Reversal, ANU 2 Dec 2025 (opened): "no structural changes will be made to the School of Music under the Renew ANU Plan."
  o Also "continuation of the School of Music in its current structure"; the new combined school "will not go ahead".
  o THE 2 Dec 2025 (opened): cuts shrank after "higher-than-expected voluntary separations"; 1,100+ submissions. https://www.timeshighereducation.com/node/740285
- Not fully reversed (Region 8 Dec 2025, opened): professional-staff realignment "will go ahead"; a student group says lost jobs stay lost. https://region.com.au/joy-anus-school-of-music-has-been-saved-but-bitterness-from-the-process-remains/926379/
  o From 2026 (Canberra Times 2 Dec 2025, opened): Performance+ Hub with one-to-one tuition, and a merged Music Practice major.
- Status late 2026: the school's page (opened, undated) says "more performance activity in the School than in the past 15 years".
  o https://music.cass.anu.edu.au/about-us . Limelight 20 Jul 2026 (paywalled, partly opened) calls the proposal "abandoned". No 2026 cuts found.
