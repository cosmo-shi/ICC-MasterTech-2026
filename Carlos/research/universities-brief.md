# Universities brief: the "university unit" buyer

- Scope: sponsors UC, Flinders, La Trobe, plus UTS; Fading Notes; comparators; the buying path.
  o All URLs accessed 6 Nov 2026. [opened] = read on the page; [snippet] or 403 = (unverified).
  o People are named only where a public unit page shows them, labelled "public unit page".

## Summary

- Confirmed: all four universities run primary teacher education; music sits inside one arts unit, never stand-alone.
  o UC 9885, Flinders EDUC2338, La Trobe EDU3009, UTS 012242; no sponsor offers a primary music specialisation.
- Confirmed: Fading Notes surveyed 36 of 49 invited institutions; average 7.98 compulsory music hours, range 1 to 19.
  o Universities are de-identified; sponsors appear only in the appendix list of degrees, and UTS is absent.
- Confirmed: no sponsor has a music school beside its teacher education; Flinders' music degree is in teach-out.
  o UC's music degree is delivered by TAFE Queensland in Queensland; La Trobe closed music in 1999 [snippet].
- Confirmed LMS: UC Canvas (UCLearn), Flinders Canvas (branded FLO since 2024), UTS Canvas; La Trobe Moodle [snippet].
- Confirmed AI: UC Copilot for students; La Trobe ChatGPT Edu to 40,000 by FY27; Flinders Gemini Enterprise, ~30,000 students.
- Confirmed path: software and LTI tools pass central IT, not the convenor alone; La Trobe adds an AI risk assessment.
- Confirmed ethics lead times: UC low-risk about 2 weeks; Flinders submit 2 months ahead; La Trobe 8 weeks [snippet].
- Not confirmed: enrolments per degree, music hours inside sponsor units, pilot prices, Alberts 4,670 / 3.82% figures.

## 2 Fading Notes detail

- Source: Fading Notes full report, June 2023, Alberts | The Tony Foundation, 48 pages [opened, text extracted].
  o https://www.alberts.co/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/Music-Education-ITE-Report_A4_130623.pdf
- Survey, exact: "Of the 49 institutions that were invited, a total of 36 responded and completed the survey" (p.10).
  o 17 lecturers joined follow-up Zoom interviews; 161 primary degrees identified from handbooks (pp.10, 13).
- De-identified, exact: "Universities have been de-identified as per the original consent agreement" (p.10).
  o Only university named in findings: Charles Darwin University, as a non-responder (p.14).
- Sponsors appear only in Appendix 1, the list of degrees considered (pp.43-44):
  o UC: B Primary Education (four versions) and Master of Primary Teaching; Flinders: B Education (Primary), M Teaching (Primary).
  o La Trobe: B Education (Primary major), M Teaching (Primary); UTS does not appear in the NSW list.
- Appendices 2 and 3 (arts units; specialisations) print headings only, so no specialism list by name.

- Key finding, exact (p.8): "Music education is taught in universities as part of the Australian Curriculum content area of the Arts, not as a standalone unit."
- Tools already used in workshops, exact (p.19): "GarageBand and Chrome Music Lab".
- Recommendations for primary music teaching degrees, exact (p.40):
  o "Increase the hours spent on music education (and all arts areas, given that there are five disciplines within one content area)."
  o "Increase the credit point value to adequately reflect the amount of work required to adequately cover all of the Arts learning area."
  o "Develop music education units that encourage growth in musical skill. These units need to allow primary teaching students to revisit/return to music throughout their degree."
  o "Ensure primary teaching students observe/participate in music lessons whilst on practicum."
  o "Advocate for K-12 teaching degrees with music specialisations."
- Recommendation for PD providers, exact (p.40), the closest fit to our app:
  o "Develop free professional learning for generalist primary teaching students that can be completed whilst they are studying their degree, and that can also count towards their degree."
- The full report sets no minimum number of hours and no mandatory stand-alone music unit.
- Alberts summary figures, about 4,670 new primary teachers a year and 3.82% with music: (unverified), summary not opened.
- ABC coverage: article not found; a repost cites "17 hours to eight" (unverified).

## 4 Buying path into a unit

| Step | Who decides | What they decide | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unit convenor | Fit into tasks; AI-use category; assessment | Convenors on UC and La Trobe public unit pages; UC 11337 GenAI only if convener authorises [opened] |
| 2 | Course director / discipline lead | Changes to unit outline or learning outcomes | Not named on course pages (unverified) |
| 3 | Learning technology / LMS team | Installs an LTI 1.3 tool; Canvas needs an admin "developer key" | Canvas LTI 1.3 release notes, https://community.canvaslms.com/docs/DOC-16794 [snippet] |
| 4 | Central IT | UC: "All IT items... corporate software purchases, regardless of cost" go via DITM; vendor questionnaire | UC Procurement Procedure cl.33-34 [opened] |
| 5 | AI risk and privacy | La Trobe: AI Adoption Risk Assessment; PIA if needed; RAAEC reviews medium/high; RAAC approves | La Trobe Responsible AI Adoption Policy [opened] |
| 6 | Procurement | UC: under $5,000 card or invoice; $5,000-$50,000 one written quote and PO; $50,000-$500,000 three suppliers | UC Procurement Procedure cl.43 [opened] |
| 7 | Human research ethics | Needed if pilot data become research; low-risk panels exist at all three sponsors | UC, Flinders, La Trobe guides below |

- Privacy law differs by state:
  o UC: ACT Information Privacy Act 2014 and Territory Privacy Principles; Privacy Act 1988 for TFNs [snippet].
  o La Trobe: Victorian Privacy and Data Protection Act 2014 [snippet, staging site].
  o UTS: NSW PPIP Act 1998; UTS states it is not directly covered by the Privacy Act 1988 [snippet].
  o Flinders: privacy policy cites the Australian Privacy Principles, Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) [snippet].
- La Trobe, exact: users "must not input, upload, transmit, or otherwise disclose any University information" into public AI; approval first.
- UC students: "free and protected access to Microsoft Copilot and Dall-E"; Turnitin AI detection enabled [opened].
  o UC units carry GenAI categories; 9865 bans GenAI, so the app must fit each unit's category.
- LTI 1.3 (1EdTech): "a technical standard (not a product)"; security via OAuth2 and JSON Web Tokens [opened].
  o LTI Advantage services: Assignment and Grade Services, Names and Role Provisioning, Deep Linking 2.0.
  o Certified products are listed in the 1EdTech TrustEd Apps Directory; certification eases IT review.
  o https://www.1edtech.org/standards/lti
- Ethics timing for a pilot that treats student data as research:
  o UC: low risk "usually assessed within two weeks"; data collection at least 2 weeks after submission (2020 guide) [opened].
  o Flinders: low-risk panel reviews out of session; submit "at least two months prior to recruitment" (July 2025) [opened].
  o La Trobe: start "eight (8) weeks in advance"; low-risk panel LEAP; governance check within 3 business days [snippet, PDF 403].
  o Studying one's own students is a dependent relationship; expect extra consent safeguards (unverified per university).
- Security signal: UTS Canvas outage after the Instructure cyber incident, May 2026; expect vendor security questions [opened].
- Cost: no public price range found for unit-level ed-tech pilots in Australia (unverified).
  o UC rule of thumb: a pilot under $5,000 needs no quote; $5,000-$50,000 needs one written quote.
