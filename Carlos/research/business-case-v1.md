## 1. Recommendation
- Pitch one buyer: the university outreach and pathway team (UC Aspire-style programmes), not the learner.
  o The learner pays nothing; the university decides whether music-led outreach is worth its own staff time.
- HEPPP is gone: discontinued 1 Jan 2026 after running 2010 to 2025 [S1] (verified again, VO).
  o Its outreach part is now the Outreach Funding Program: about $44m a year, paid to Table A universities [S2][S3].
  o Never say "HEPPP-funded"; say "fits the university's Outreach Funding" (decisions-log, 6 Nov).
- Published 2026 Outreach Funding grants: UC $384,182, Flinders $902,208, La Trobe $1,419,938; national total $43,735,160 [S7].
  o Determination 5812, approved 10 Feb 2026, "Initial 2026 funding"; 2027 amounts not located.
  o A published allocation is not an uncommitted budget and not a purchasing decision; no buyer has agreed yet.
  o Guidance allows rollover into 2027 [S3, VO]; a 2027 pilot rides on rollover or 2027 amounts. Acquittal for 2026 funds is 27 March 2027.
  o Scale, our arithmetic: A$5,000 is 1.30% of UC's 2026 grant; the full all-in estimate in section 3 is 13.5%.
- Catch: Outreach Funding must build aspiration for higher education, must not be recruitment, and cannot fund enrolled-student support [S3].
  o The app rides inside an outreach activity (music hook, university students as hand-off mentors, a pathway conversation), not a stand-alone music app.
  o The pilot measures interest in further study separately from enjoyment of music, or it does not count as outreach.
- Fallback ask, same slide: a one-unit pilot in an education-faculty arts unit (UC 9885, Flinders EDUC2338, La Trobe EDU3009; unit codes are assumptions pending current handbooks) [universities-brief.md].
- Consumer and tutor routes stay on the vision slide only (section 4).
- Cisco: a small, true fit only (the hand-off could run over Webex; today a mock). No Cisco grant or partnership is agreed [cisco-fit-note.md s1, s5].

## 3. What it takes to proceed
### 3a. The ask
- A 12-week pilot in one outreach programme (preferred) or one arts unit, starting in the 2027 school year.
- Why 12 weeks: a design choice to measure past the first weeks; the claimed dip after week 4 is unverified [gamification-research.md].
- A better retake shows immediate change only; add a delayed re-test at week 12 for retention.

- A$1 = US$0.6900, RBA, 6 Nov 2026 [S28], so US$1 = A$1.449.
- Sessions per learner: 3 a week x 12 weeks = 36 sessions (assumption). Months: 12 weeks = 2.77 months.
- LLM per session, tech-feedback-layer.md section 6 (Sonnet 5.5 and Haiku 5.5 prices opened there 2026-11-06): all Sonnet US$0.095; routed US$0.043; all Haiku US$0.014; offline US$0.

### 3d. Time and sequence
- Ethics: a written determination before any recruiting (nine-student-test-plan.md s1: control groups, a cohort comparison and a prototype are three NHMRC review triggers).
  o A university-run pilot with minors needs human research ethics review if it is research; classification comes first.
  o Lead time 2 weeks (UC low risk) to 2 months (Flinders); La Trobe 8 weeks (snippet) [universities-brief.md s4]. Planning assumptions, not verified.
  o School-system and guardian consent for minors: per state and per school; no lead time found.
- Order: eligibility and budget owner, then ethics determination and contract and privacy review, then school permissions, then recruitment, then 12 weeks. The preparation can take longer than the pilot.
- Privacy: the sponsor is expected to be data controller; the vendor processes the features; audio stays on the device by default; sharing a recording is a per-recording opt-in with an expiring link (privacy-design-v0.md s1). Agreements are needed before launch.

### 3e. Split of work
- University supplies: one programme or unit, a coordinator, consent and ethics path, school contacts, hand-off mentors.
- We supply: the app, set-up, a one-hour coordinator briefing, a weekly data summary.
- We measure: weekly active learners; retakes with a measured fix; hand-offs offered and taken; week-10 retention; confidence; interest in further study, asked separately from enjoyment of music (mapped to the SEHEEF evaluation principle, not a promise that it satisfies the report).
- Feedback quality check before launch: a qualified teacher scores a sample of the app's fixes as right or wrong across phones, rooms and voices.
- Go or no-go at week 12 (retakes show immediate change; the week-12 delayed re-test shows retention): go if most learners still practise in weeks 8 to 12, most retakes show a measured fix and hand-offs happen; no-go if use collapses or the fix does not hold on retake. Thresholds are set with the sponsor; none invented.
