## 3. Evidence that holds up
| Finding | Use |
|---|---|
| Hint-only tutor lifted practice 127% and removed the 17% exam harm of open GPT-4 (PNAS 2025, about 1,000 students) | Direction beats answers; the strongest single line |
| 49% of 6,960 students at four Australian universities already use GenAI for feedback (2025) | The behaviour exists; we shape it |
| Flinders requires a prompt-and-output appendix for AI-allowed tasks | A sponsor already collects process; nobody makes it readable |
| ACU: nearly 6,000 alleged cases in 2024, about 90% AI, detector dropped; Sydney: detection "not possible" | Detection is dead; proof of learning is the replacement |
| Only 12% of 3,400 Australian academics saw real productivity gains from AI | Never claim staff time savings without a trial |
| OAIC: no personal data into public AI tools; House inquiry: avoid offshore storage | Campus-hosted or licensed models, opt-in, no device access |
| Gamification in HE: small engagement gains, mixed learning gains (meta-analyses, to cite) | Claim engagement and visibility, prove learning in a pilot |

## 4. Where the incumbents leave a gap
- No product maps a student's work to the unit's learning outcomes (Studiosity, Cogniti, Cognito, Clarity checked).
- No product gives the student a private view first; in all of them the institution sees the work.
- Every verified incumbent runs in a vendor cloud; universities are already leaving (RMIT, UNE).
- The AI-use card is not unique (Studiosity Validate, Turnitin Clarity). The tutor is crowded (three apps).
- Cognito proves AI-marked short answers lift grades at school level; nobody does that per university outcome.

## 5. How MasterTech winners won (2024 and 2025, both Innovation Central Canberra)
- One user at one decision point: shopper at the shelf, shopper in the aisle.
- Built on the sponsor's existing platform: Coles app, Flybuys, purchase history. Judges' top word: feasibility.
- Opened with an emotional phrase, "walking wallets", before any technology.
- Flipped the sponsor's default metric: plastic over price, presence over spend.
- One worked example a judge could repeat: four small Coke bottles become two large.
- AI in the background, never the headline; no Cisco product named by either winner.
- Social impact built in; a funded next step; the 2025 winner was itself a quest.

## 6. How hackathon winners read a broad question (19 cases)
- Rewrite it as "who, stuck at what moment, changes how". Alcademy narrowed smart education to procrastination.
- Move the user one step from the obvious: teachers (Dyscover), parents (EmpowerIEP). Judges: "beyond the typical ed-tech approach".
- Take the verb literally: "learn" became explain and practise (Maieutic, MedKit), not receive answers.
- Uniqueness is a point of view or a smart combination, not a stack. Dyscover ran on Teachable Machine.
- Judges compare you with the room: a fourth tutor scores as the fourth tutor.
- The score is set in the first 30 seconds: what it is, for whom. Demo, not slides.
- Sponsor as customer: "if I reward this, will my company look good?" One number from a real test.
- Tutor entries placed third in the cases checked; none won.

## 8. Three candidate shapes against the filter
| Shape | One line | Fails or strains |
|---|---|---|
| A. Questline | AI turns any unit outline into a map with regions, bosses, side quests, parties and a chronicle; no content changes | T2 borderline (quests can look like a quiz app); T5 only if quests are direction-only; "chocolate-covered broccoli" risk |
| B. The Apprentice | Each student teaches an AI apprentice the unit; it only knows what you can explain; its mastery is your outcome map; the cohort's apprentices share misconceptions | Trend check 5 Nov: NOT new. Axiom Flow (live at one university, Moodle LTI) is the nearest product; five LLM tutee systems since 2023. Novel combination is defensible, "first" is not. Evidence thin, samples 20 to 41 |
| C. Learning Radar | "Lost" taps in lectures plus weekly checks feed a private map and a cohort outbreak view, with boosters | T4 phrase still needed; depends on lecture capture consent; strongest evidence base of the three |

- Shape B, how it works:
  o The student explains a concept to their apprentice; the apprentice tries the week's problem using only that explanation.
  o Where the apprentice fails, the student sees the gap in their own understanding. That is the stuck map.
  o Teaching log is the student-owned record; markers see it only on opt-in; it is the Flinders appendix made readable.
  o Why not Claude: Claude already knows the answer; an apprentice that knows only what you taught it cannot be faked by pasting.
  o Evidence base: learning by teaching and the protégé effect (Chase, Chin, Oppezzo and Schwartz 2009; Betty's Brain). [evidence needed: a 2023 to 2026 study of LLM teachable agents in higher education]
  o Flips the metric: you are assessed on how well you taught, which is how well you understand.
  o Journey built in: your apprentice levels up through the unit; parties co-teach; the cohort's apprentices reveal shared misconceptions.
  o Makes students think more (Frank), learn with humans (Chris), maps to outcomes (John), answers "learn" literally (hackathon pattern).
- Shape A and B combine cleanly: the apprentice is the companion on the Questline map; its gaps are the fog.
- Shape C supplies cohort intelligence to either A or B.

## 9. Where the research points
- The answer is a reversal, not a tutor: AI that makes students answer, not AI that answers students.
- The student is the hero and the thinker; AI structures, asks, mirrors and records. It never writes the deliverable.
- The unit's learning outcomes are the spine; the student's demonstrated understanding per outcome is the metric.
- The experience has a shape and a story (a journey), which both 2025 winners' DNA and Carlos's lead support.
- It rides on what universities already run and license; nothing to install, no keys, data stays on campus.
- Process becomes evidence a student owns and chooses to share; detection and surveillance are out.
- The pitch opens with a phrase for how students feel now, names one student in one week, and shows one 60-second path.
- The ask is a one-unit pilot at a sponsor university with a cost and a measure, plus an inclusion lever.
- Trend check done (trend-check-teachable-agents.md): teach-the-AI exists; our edge must be the outcome map, student-owned log and cohort patterns.
- Owed before Friday: a teacher-qualified validator and a nine-student test plan.
