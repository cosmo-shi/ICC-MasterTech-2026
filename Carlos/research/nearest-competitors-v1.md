## 0. Bottom line

- Nearest product: Mastery: AI Audio Coach (Waveform Systems, App Store v1.4). It scores a recording, pins notes to timestamps, re-analyses after practice, and lets a reviewer comment by link, free.
  o Runner-up on the human side: SmartMusic and PracticeFirst (teacher sees the recording and score); on the note-level side: Noteflight SoundCheck.
- Honest gap: no opened page shows one fix in plain words, a retake check on the same phrase, and a hand-off aimed at learners who have no teacher.
  o Three parts overlap in part: re-analysis (Mastery), teacher review (Mastery, SmartMusic, PracticeFirst, Between Lessons, ArtistWorks), error locations with retry (SoundCheck).
  o Pages are marketing text; "not shown" is not "not built". We have not run Mastery.
- 40-word judge answer (34 words): "Mastery scores a recording and lets a reviewer comment by link. Its page does not show one plain-words fix or a same-phrase retake check. We add those, plus a teacher at the ceiling." Fallback (15 words): see section 4.
- Three checks before Wednesday (10 minutes each, section 5): run Mastery's three free analyses on one phrase twice; read Mastery's privacy and age terms; read SoundCheck and SmartMusic help pages in a browser.

## 4. The answer when a judge says "Mastery already does this"

- Mastery, 34 spoken words (v1): "Mastery scores a recording and lets a reviewer comment by link. Its page does not show one plain-words fix or a same-phrase retake check. We add those, plus a teacher at the ceiling."
  o Codex critique (kept): "We add those" can sound like verified exclusivity, and it hides that Mastery has re-analysis and free review.
- Mastery, safer v2, 39 words: "Mastery offers re-analysis and review by link from a reviewer the learner brings. Our claimed difference is one explained fix, a same-phrase retake check, and a teacher hand-off when the learner is stuck. Its page does not show those."
  o Changed 7 Nov: "free human review" became "review by link from a reviewer the learner brings" (listing, 22).
  o A fact-check offered a 36-word answer that drops the "page does not show" caveat and leans on outreach and mentors; not adopted, the claimed difference stays testable.
- SmartMusic or SoundCheck, 33 words: "SmartMusic and SoundCheck score notes for a class and send the result to the class teacher. We add one plain-words fix, a same-phrase retake check, and a teacher for learners who have none."
- Fallback if pushed, 15 words: "Their page shows no same-phrase retake check; that, plus the teacher handoff, is our difference."
- Likely follow-ups, safe replies (Codex, kept):
  o "Isn't re-analysis a retake?" Reply: "Yes; the page does not say it measures improvement on the same phrase."
  o "Doesn't Mastery have a human reviewer?" Reply: "Yes; our narrower claim is a hand-off when stuck, with the fix history."
  o "Does it work for teacherless learners through institutions?" Reply: "Not shown yet; that is our target and plan, not a result."
  o Say "our claimed difference", not "our difference", until product tests exist.
- Rules kept: no "first", "only" or "nobody"; the claim stays "the combination"; "page does not show" is said, not "cannot".
  o Do not say "Mastery has no human review"; it has a free link-review mechanism, with the learner supplying the reviewer.
  o SmartMusic hears by microphone and MIDI, so never imply that hearing the notes is our difference.
