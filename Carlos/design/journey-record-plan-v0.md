# Journey record plan v0: 15 minutes a day, Saturday 7 to Wednesday 11 Nov

- Date: Fri 6 Nov 2026 (overnight draft for the team; verify before the pitch). Closes judge-lens-v1.md section 5 item 7.
- Purpose: make sure a true record of the week exists whether or not the organiser scores it.
- Scope: what to capture, who, when, how to name it, what never goes in. No new tools; a phone and the existing folder.
- Status: nothing here changes the build, the pitch or the freeze. Every capture is a copy or a photo, never a task on the critical path.

## 1. Bottom line

- Minimum record (all of it fits in 75 minutes across five days):
  o One photo or screenshot a day, one clip (the retake, section 4), and one dated line per day saying what changed and why.
  o Six named stills plus the clip are enough to build the 60-second video (section 5) or the one-page alternative (section 5.2) on Tuesday night.
  o If nothing else happens, the file `journey/journey-log.md` with five dated lines is still a real record.
- Owner: a role, the "journey keeper". The team picks the person on Saturday morning.
  o Pick a teammate who is not the build operator on Saturday (the operator is busy recording take 1, playbook P4.1).
  o The keeper captures, names files and writes the daily line; everyone else only tells the keeper "that was a decision".
  o The keeper has the right to say "stop, photo" for 30 seconds, no more.
- Question for the organiser on Monday 9 Nov (questions-for-sponsor.md row 44, quoted exactly):
  o "Programme staff: the info pack says teams "document their journey" (p4) and past editions had a three-minute journey video; is a journey record required in 2026, in what form, by when, and does it count toward the score?"
  o Ask it at the 8 am session. The answer decides section 5.1 (video) or 5.2 (page) on Monday morning, not Tuesday night.
- The pack gives no format for the journey (key-facts-info-pack.md section 2). So plan for both, build the cheaper first.

## 3. The daily capture table (cap 15 minutes a day)

- Naming pattern: `journey/YYYY-MM-DD_NN_shot_what.ext`, for example `journey/2026-11-07_02_S4_retake-clip.mp4`.
  o `S1` to `S6` tags map each capture to a shot in section 5; `--` if it maps to none.
- Consent rules that apply to every row:
  o No faces of anyone under 18. If in doubt about age, treat the person as under 18 and shoot backs, hands or screens.
  o An adviser, a learner or any visitor in a photo is asked first. The log notes the role, the uses agreed (team folder, submitted video, stage) and "may withdraw before Wed 17:00"; a yes to a photo is not a yes to publicity.
  o Backs, hands, voices and names visible on a screen can still identify a person; crop or blur before use.
  o Organiser material: permission to photograph is separate from permission to show it publicly; confirm before it enters the submitted record.
  o Photos of organiser material (success criteria, slides) only after asking staff; otherwise photograph our own notes.
  o The nine-student test follows its own consent step (nine-student-test-plan-v1.md section 3): no photo, no audio, no video there; the journey gets only the denominator and one sentence.
  o Never film visitors at the stand; ask the venue about filming rules (playbook P6.4, P12).

| Day | Capture 1 | Capture 2 | Capture 3 | Who | Minutes | Consent and limits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sat 7 Nov (build day) | Whiteboard or paper sketch of the one learner moment (`S1`) | Screen recording of take 1, the cue and the retake on the real build (section 4, `S4`) | Photo of the options page with crossed-out ideas (`S2`), plus one decision line: the demo phrase or the one-learner choice, with the reason | Keeper shoots; operator records the clip | 15 | No faces; clip carries only a teammate's own voice with their yes, or is screen-only |
| Sun 8 Nov (travel, 6 pm dinner) | Photo of the printed script with the first timing log (`S3`) | Screenshot of the speaker split | One line: what we cut and why | Keeper | 10 | No photos at the dinner or on the bus; script and screens only |

| Tue 10 Nov (surprise task; freeze 15:00) | Our own note of the surprise task and how we answered it (`S5`) | Screenshot of the frozen build tag and the re-recorded clip v2 (`S3`) | One line: what we did NOT build after 15:00 and why | Keeper; operator hands over clip v2 | 15 (assembly in 5.1 is extra, see below) | Surprise task wording only if the organiser allows it; test evening 2 gives one line, no media |

- Total planned time: 65 minutes across five days; every day stays under the cap.
- Assembly (video or page) is a separate team task with its own owner (not the keeper alone) and deadline: Tuesday 18:15 to 18:45, or the 19:00 bus if the phone editor allows.
  o Captions, voice-over, export and a playback check make 20 minutes optimistic; 30 is planned. It is the only item above 15 minutes in a day.
  o Cut to the page version if the Tuesday fix list is long. A final Wednesday export is optional, only before the stand opens.
- Log claims need evidence: "frozen at 15:00" needs a timestamped tag or log line; "tried" means tested, otherwise write "considered".
- Rule of thumb for the keeper: if a capture takes over five minutes, take the photo and write the line, nothing more.

## 4. The failing-retake clip as the centrepiece

- Why: judge-lens-v1.md section 5 item 3 asks for one repeatable worked example, "including a retake that does not improve and what the app then does".
  o The Wednesday pitch needs one true worked example; this clip is the only part of the record that can carry it.
- What to capture Saturday, on the real build and the real laptop (the same chain as clip v1, playbook P4.1):
  o Take 1 with the known error, with the screen showing the live line and the measured number.
  o The one cue the app gives (one fix, as written on screen).
  o The retake, with the number shown again.
  o If a retake fails to improve or the app flags it, keep that take too and the app's next step; do not delete it.
  o A failure may not occur. Record what did happen; if no retake failed, the log says "failure case not observed".
  o Write the metric, its direction and the comparison rule in the log next to the number; a bare number proves nothing.
  o For S4 use stills or a 10-second excerpt of the 20 to 30 second sequence.
- What the screen must show and how it is labelled:

| Element | Rule |
|---|---|
| On-screen text | Only what the build prints; add no caption of our own inside the picture |
| Labels | Three captions added outside the picture, in the file name or an edit: "Take 1", "Cue", "Retake" |
| Length | 20 to 30 seconds per sequence; one file per sequence; keep the raw file untouched and work on a copy |
| Phrase and device | The frozen demo phrase `[PHRASE]` once locked (freeze playbook P2.1); say "phrase not yet locked" in the log until then |
| Audio | A teammate's own voice with their yes, or a silent screen recording; no learner or visitor audio |
| Second copy | Copy to a second device the same day (playbook P2.1, recording row) |

- Claims the clip supports and does not support:
  o Supports: "one user, one phrase, one attempt and one retake; the retake compares to take 1 on this number".
  o Does not support: a learning gain, a general accuracy, any claim about other learners or other phrases.
  o If the clip shows a retake that did not improve, say so in the log line; an honest failure is the example.
  o Saturday's clip may be replaced by Tuesday's clip v2 only if the build was fixed; keep both, label them v1 and v2.

## 5. The 60-second journey video (if required) and the one-page alternative

### 5.1 Video outline: six shots, ten seconds each

| Shot | Seconds | What it shows | Capture used | Voice-over line (one line, plain) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | 0-10 | The one learner moment: the sketch or whiteboard, and the problem in one sentence | Sat capture 1 | "This is the one moment we built for." |
| S2 | 10-20 | What we tried and dropped: two or three crossed-out options on paper | Sat decision line, Sun cut line | "We tried these and dropped them because..." |
| S3 | 20-30 | The build: screen of the working loop, the timing log, the frozen tag | Sun script photo, Tue tag and clip v2 | "By Tuesday at 3 pm it was frozen." |
| S4 | 30-40 | The retake: Take 1, Cue, Retake, with the number on screen | Sat clip (section 4) | "Take 1, one cue, one retake, one measured change." |
| S5 | 40-50 | The change of mind: criteria mapping sheet and the surprise-task note | Mon sheet, Tue note | "The criteria and the surprise task changed this." |
| S6 | 50-60 | The stand with the screen and the what-next list, no faces | Mon stand photo and the business-case next step (Wed photo swaps in at a final Wed export if time allows) | "Proposed next step: one pilot, if a buyer agrees." |

- Make it from stills and one clip in a phone editor; no music track at all unless its rights are clear (use silence or a voice-over).
- Voice-over: recorded once by a teammate with their yes; keep it to six lines.
- Test the video once on the venue screen only if the organiser asks for it; never promise it in the pitch.
- If the Monday answer is "three minutes": the six shots grow to about 18 shots of 10 seconds; list the extra captures Monday and capture them Monday to Tuesday.
  o TODO: a full three-minute outline is not written; write it only if the organiser confirms the requirement.

### 5.2 One-page written alternative (if video is not required)

| Heading | Content (one or two lines each) | Capture used |
|---|---|---|
| The moment | The one learner moment and who it is for | Sat sketch |
| What we tried first | Options considered and dropped | Sat and Sun lines |
| What failed | The retake that did not improve, or the cue that was unclear | Section 4 clip, log line |
| What changed and why | The criteria change, the surprise task, the freeze | Mon and Tue lines |
| What we tested | The denominator actually reached (for example "n of nine finished"), no more | Test day lines |
| What next | The one actionable next step from the business case | Wed line |

- One page, six headings, dated lines pasted from `journey/journey-log.md`; screenshots only where they fit.

## 6. Folder and file naming

- Everything lives in `09 - MasterTech Challenge 2026/journey/`. Nothing goes anywhere else: not a phone album shared outside, not a cloud folder.

| File | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Stills | `YYYY-MM-DD_NN_Sx_what.jpg` | `journey/2026-11-07_01_S1_learner-moment-sketch.jpg` |
| Clips | `YYYY-MM-DD_NN_Sx_what.mp4`, raw untouched, edits get `_v2` | `journey/2026-11-07_02_S4_retake-clip.mp4` |
| Daily line | one file `journey/journey-log.md`, one dated line per day, newest last | `2026-11-07: decided phrase X because ...; asked, said yes` |
| Output | `journey/journey-video-60s.mp4` or `journey/journey-page.md` | built Tue night |

- What never goes in `journey/`:
  o Names outside the allowed list (advisers: Jeff, Frank, Rose, Chris, Scott, Rodney, John, Stanley, Ivan, Dan, Jane, Kat, Madison; teammates Simone, Justin; "the mentor" unnamed; Carlos). Visitors, judges and test participants appear as roles or not at all.
  o Learner audio, visitor audio, any recording from the nine-student test, any face of an under-18.
  o Anything from the rest of the internship folder: files, captures, screenshots of lab tools, device names, addresses, credentials.
  o Photos of organiser material the staff did not clear; the Participant Agreement's confidentiality wording is not in hand (key-facts open question).
- Keep the folder out of the public repo until the team decides: Carlos runs git and decides what is shared.
