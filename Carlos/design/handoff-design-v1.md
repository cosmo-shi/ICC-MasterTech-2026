- Date: Fri 6 Nov 2026 (AEDT), overnight. Purpose: the end-to-end teacher hand-off (R10), mockable in a day, sayable in 30 seconds, safe to defend.
- Recommendation: offer a human at the third valid miss on one cause (or any time the learner asks); share one chosen clip and the fix list by expiring link; a student reviewer drafts, a qualified music supervisor approves; one reply, no chat; a safeguarding path sits behind every case.

## 1. Recommendation (conclusion first)
- Trigger: the miss rule from tech-feedback-layer.md s3, counting only valid comparable takes; miss 3 on one cause = hand-off offer; the learner may ask for a human at any time.
  o Plus four more ceilings (tired twice, a `ceiling` rule, learner asks, needs eyes); pain or strain is an immediate stop, not a hand-off.
- Card: one chosen 10 to 30 second clip, the goal sentence, the fixes tried with before-and-after; pseudonymous id ("Learner 12"); no name, no contact details.
- Recipients, stated plainly: one student reviewer, the supervisor, and safeguarding staff if a concern is raised.
- Reviewer: one screen; replies with a draft cue, a short voice note, or a lesson-offer request; the supervisor approves every reply.
  o Reviewer may answer "cannot assess": no duty to invent a cue.
- Supply for a 12-week pilot: pre-service teachers (UC 9885, Flinders EDUC2338, La Trobe EDU3009) as a supervised pool; each holds a check in the learner's state; a qualified music educator supervises.
  o Gives the future teacher real music practice: 58% of surveyed universities say students never see a music lesson on practicum (state-of-play s5, Fading Notes 2023).
- Safety: the named child-safety officer, an escalation path (6.1), guardian permission under 15 and a guardian copy offered at 15 to 17 (programme policy, matching privacy-design-v1 s3), a child who can decline or withdraw even when a guardian agrees, a share log, urgent harm goes to 000 and police first.
- Pitch fix: the pitch card says "Next is hand position"; audio cannot show hands. Use an audio ceiling on stage (section 9).

## 2. Trigger rule (ceiling signals)
- Rule source: tech-feedback-layer.md s3 (miss 1 same cue; miss 2 easier step; miss 3 hand-off) and its HAND-OFF clause. The engine sets `cue.ceiling` and `cue.handoff_flag` (take-data-contract 1.4).
- Conflict kept in the log: build-plan-v0.md s7 says "same cue fired three sessions in a row"; this file follows the tech file (miss 3), as briefed. See Open questions.
| # | Signal | Threshold | What the app says |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Same cause, no pass | Miss 3 on one cause over valid comparable takes; a `counts` false take (noise, empty, wrong difficulty) is not a miss; count expires after 14 days idle (proposed); resets on a pass | "We've tried this three times. A teacher can hear what's getting in the way." |
| T2 | Tired voice returns | V1 fires in 2 sessions | "Your voice tired early twice. A teacher can help with your practice plan." (suggests, never diagnoses; pain stays T6) |
| T3 | Technique beyond a cue | A rule marked `ceiling` fires | "This note needs more than one tip. A teacher can show you how to build up to it." |
| T4 | Learner asks, any time | Taps "Ask a teacher", or says "I can't do this" (R2) | "Good call. A person can find you a smaller first step." Offer a break, a smaller step or a human; do not treat frustration as a disclosure |
| T5 | Needs eyes or set-up help | Words mention hands, posture, how the instrument is held, or a set-up barrier that persists | "I can't see that from a recording. A teacher can look with you in a lesson." Programme help is offered; absolute "never hand off" dropped |
| T6 | Pain or strain, stop | Learner words mention pain, hoarseness or hurt | "Stop for today and tell an adult you trust. If it still hurts, see a doctor." No easier retake. Severe breathing difficulty means call 000. No health advice beyond this |
- Precedence: T6 beats all; T2 to T5 fire on any take; T1 fires only at miss 3.
- Never a hand-off: M1 (cannot hear), M2 (device delay) on first occurrence: set-up fix first.
- T6 is a stop: no audio rule detects pain; the app reacts to what the learner says.
- Why the changes: three misses can arrive too early (noise, wrong difficulty) or too late (strain waits for repeats); validity filter, pain stop and any-time human cover both.

### Consent wording (learner, about 60 words)
- "Send this 20-second clip, your goal and the fixes we tried? A student teacher and their supervisor will see them, not your name or contact details. A safety officer sees them if a concern is raised. The link stops working after 7 days, or sooner if you cancel. We can delete it on request, but keep a safety record if one exists. [Send] [Not now]"
  o Preview shows the exact payload, recipients, guardian visibility, expiry and how to complain; refusing costs nothing.
  o Tip: "Don't say your name or where you live in the clip." Voices can still identify a person; the supervisor screens first (section 4).

### Guardian loop for under 18 (programme policy, not a claimed legal rule)
- Basis: OAIC says the Privacy Act "doesn't specify an age"; capacity is case by case; an entity may generally assume capacity over 15; a guardian may consent where maturity is lacking [6]. Everything below is the pilot's own safeguard.
| Step | Rule |
|---|---|
| Who | Programme coordinator verifies parental responsibility from the enrolment form; relevant orders or safe-contact limits noted; child assent asked for |
| When | Permission at enrolment and for each share; separate permission for lessons, research use or a changed recipient set |
| Under 15 | Guardian taps each share and sees the reply; child sees the preview |
| 15 to 17 | Learner taps if capable; guardian copy offered, not forced; concerns go to the child-safety officer |
| Record | Verified consenter, child assent, wording version, time, case, recipients, withdrawal |
| Guardian refuses | No share, no penalty; offer practice-only and an easier goal; never bypass |
| Unsafe or unavailable guardian | Child-safety officer decides; no clip share until decided. This blocks only the optional teacher share, never a safety report (6.1) |
| Child declines | A child can decline or withdraw even when a guardian approves; practice access is unaffected |
- Link back to privacy-design-v1.md s3.1 (ten-step loop): this section adds assent, preview and refusal; nothing leaves the phone before the tap.

### What is shared, what is not
| Shared (this case only) | Never shared |
|---|---|
| One chosen clip, goal sentence, cues tried with proof, instrument, age band, pseudonymous case id | Name, email, phone, school, suburb, other takes, account ID, progress history |

### After sending, and when nobody answers
| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| Sent | Status: Sent, Being looked at, Reply ready |
| 48 hours with no reviewer reply | Case moves to the supervisor's queue; supervisor answers or reassigns |
| 72 hours | Learner told it is taking longer; easier small goal offered (tech file next_goal_hint) |
| Day 7 | Link expires; learner told in plain words that no reply came; the clip stays on the phone; no new case until a new ceiling and consent |
- No reply is counted and reported (metrics, section 7); the app never tells a learner a teacher is "listening" before someone has opened it.
- Cancel sharing: kills the link, deletes the cloud copy, logs it. Honest limit: cannot undo a listen that already happened.

## 4. Reviewer one screen and the reply path
### Who the reviewer is and what they must have before week 1
| Requirement | Rule |
|---|---|
| Role | Pre-service teacher in an approved placement or volunteer programme; supervised pool |
| Check | Holds a valid check in the learner's state (ACT WWVP, Victoria WWCC, NSW WWCC, SA WWCC). Coordinator verifies identity, category, status and link to the programme with the issuer, not a pasted number (online verification routes `unverified`) |
| Why online counts | ACT Act and Victoria page name oral and electronic contact; NSW lists online mentoring [2][4][3]. Pilot rule: check anyway, even where a state might exempt |
| Training | Child-safety induction, code of conduct (no private contact, no social media, no personal details), disclosure and concern steps (6.1), "cannot assess" practice |
| Supervision | Qualified music educator approves every reply; caps cases per reviewer |
| Not allowed | Opening a link without sign-in; saving the clip; contacting the learner or guardian; guessing identity |

### The one screen (no scrolling on a laptop)
| Block | Content |
|---|---|
| Header | Case id, learner ref ("Learner 12"), instrument, age band, state, "expires in N days" |
| Goal and why | Goal sentence; trigger row in plain words |
| Clip | 10 to 30 s player; streamed; no download button (copying cannot be prevented, e.g. screen capture) |
| Fixes tried | Each cue with before-and-after numbers |
| Ask | "What is the one next thing?" or [Cannot assess] |
| Concern | [Raise a concern] always visible (6.1) |
- Supervisor screens goal text and clip before the reviewer sees them (identifying details, concern, audio quality). Adds time; measure it.

### Three replies, time limits and return path
| Reply | Limit | Learner gets | Return into the app |
|---|---|---|---|
| Written cue | One sentence, external focus | Next cue, tagged "from your teacher" after approval | Stored as cue with `source` = teacher; retake shows whether the next take improved, not that the cue caused it |
| Voice note | 30 s, one take (stretch, not mock) | Plays on the card | Same retake proof |
| Lesson offer | Programme booking only | "Your teacher suggests a lesson. Your coordinator will contact your parent or carer." | Case closes; booking via programme |
| Cannot assess | One line | "We could not tell from this clip. Try a new take or ask for a lesson." | No cue invented |
- Time limits, one clock from the learner's send, in working days (weekends and absences excluded, proposals): supervisor screens within 1; reviewer drafts by 2; supervisor approves by 3; unanswered cases are closed by the supervisor; one reply per case, no thread. The section 3 timings (48 hours, 72 hours) are read on this clock.
- Reject a reply if it: asks for identifying details, suggests contact outside the app, or tells the learner to push through pain.
- Closing the loop: the reviewer sees "improved / not yet" after the retake.

### 6.1 Escalation path: a disclosure or concern
- Scope: a clip, goal text or reply that suggests a child is being harmed, groomed or contacted unsafely; or a reviewer behaving wrongly. Practice below is the pilot's proposal. The AIFS practice guide says disclosures go to the state or territory reporting authority and organisations need protocols [11]; the exact steps below are `unverified` against it (page returned 403).
- Gaps from the Codex re-check, now rules: reviewers get a staffed escalation channel with an out-of-hours number and a named deputy; if the child-safety officer or supervisor is implicated or unreachable, the reviewer goes to the university backup or straight to the external authority; nobody waits for internal approval to meet a personal duty. A restricted safeguarding record may hold the exact words and, if needed, the clip; it is separate from the share log and its access and retention are set by the officer.
| Step | Who | What |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Immediate danger | Anyone | Call 000 or police; for online grooming or unwanted contact with a child the ACCCE takes reports, but not in place of 000 when a child is in immediate danger [10] |
| 2 Reviewer stops | Reviewer | Do not reply, investigate, ask the child anything, or contact the family; note case id and the exact words; raise [Raise a concern] and tell the supervisor the same day |
| 3 Triage | Child-safety officer | Assess risk; identify the learner through the coordinator-held mapping (reviewers stay pseudonymous); avoid alerting a guardian who may be implicated |
| 4 Report | Child-safety officer | Report to the state child-protection authority or police where thresholds are met; an internal referral never replaces a person's own duty |
| 5 Hold | Programme | Freeze the share link and keep the evidence record; restrict access of anyone implicated |
| 6 Tell | Programme | Update the learner or guardian where safe; give an external route (state commissioner, eSafety) |
- Mandatory-reporting duties differ by state, role and threshold (belief in the ACT and Victoria, suspicion in NSW, per AIFS snippets) [12]; whether pre-service students or volunteers are mandated is `unverified`. Mapping this is the sponsor's task before launch.
- A reviewer who is the subject of a concern: separate route to the child-safety officer and a backup (university officer) so the child-safety officer is not the only door.
- Learner complaint route: the button, a named officer and an independent backup, in plain words; written at about age 10 to 12 level.

## 7. Metrics and the stage slide
### Pilot metrics (proposed targets, to agree with the sponsor; no results yet)
| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hand-offs offered, share taken | Count; no target | Business case metric |
| Median time to reply | 48 hours or less | Learner still remembers the take |
| Answered within 48 hours; expired unanswered | 70% or more; count | Honest about no-reply |
| Answered within 72 hours | 80% or more | Supply works |
| Retake within 7 days of a reply | 50% or more | Reply re-entered practice |
| Retake after a teacher cue improves on its own proof | Report the share, worsened included | Signal, not causal proof |
| Handling time per case (reviewer + supervisor + rework) | Report median; planning figure 5 plus 1 minute is an assumption | Cost model |
| "Cannot assess" and rejected replies | Count | Quality gate |
| Safety | Zero contact-detail exchanges; concerns acknowledged in 1 business day; urgent concerns actioned at once; every cancel and deletion logged | Child safety |
- Stop rules for the 12 weeks: pause the pilot if a safeguarding failure occurs (a contact-detail exchange or a missed urgent concern) until the child-safety officer clears it.
- Stage slide: three panels (learner card, reviewer screen, reply with the retake proof). Headline: "The app listens every day. A person steps in where a human matters." Footer: the time targets, labelled "pilot targets".

## 9. Thirty seconds, the honest limits and claims not to make
### Spoken (about 70 words; critique wording adopted with edits)
- "Our proposed pilot offers human help when practice stalls, or when a learner asks. With the learner's agreement, and a guardian's permission for under-15s, they share one chosen clip and fix list. A student drafts a reply; a qualified music teacher approves it. Replies stay in the app. Staffing and response times are untested targets."
### 40-word safety answer
- "We propose verified screening, trained reviewers, approval of every reply, child assent and guardian permission for minors. Access expires, contact stays in the app, and concerns reach safeguarding staff. Required safety records may be kept. These controls reduce risk; they cannot guarantee safety." (about 43 words)
### On-stage card wording (replaces "hand position")
- "Third try, still pushing on the high note. A teacher can help with this part. Share this clip and your fix list with a pilot teacher?"
### Claims not to make
| Do not say | Why | Say instead |
|---|---|---|
| "The coach hears the same problem" as causal hearing | It matches rules, not a human ear | "It tracks the same miss" |
| "Anonymous", "no one can identify the child" | Voice and goal text can identify | "No name or contact details; a pseudonymous id" |
| "Fully deleted" | Links expire; copies and safeguarding records may remain | "Deleted on request, except a safety record if one exists" |
| "A teacher usually replies in 2 days" | No pilot result | "We aim to reply within 3 working days" |
| "Checked teachers" with no process | Checks need verifying | "Reviewers with a verified check, proposed" |
| "Approved work placement means insured" | Cover unconfirmed | "Insurance to be confirmed in writing" |
| "Compliant", "legal", "safe" | Not claimed | "Designed to reduce risk" |
### The one honest limitation
- "A recording can't show hands or posture, and our teachers are a proposed pilot: until a university runs it, the reply time is a target, not a result."
