# Carmen pilot: what to measure, and questions for the sponsor

Nothing here has been measured yet. These are the measures to collect in a first school pilot and
the decisions the team needs from the sponsor before one. Carmen makes no claim about learning
outcomes; a pilot of this size could not support one.

## Purpose of a first pilot

Find out whether a class teacher who is not a music specialist can run a 20-minute Carmen session
with a class, and whether learners can use it without an adult fixing things. Not: whether it
improves musical learning.

## Measures

Counted from the app's own data only where that data already exists for teaching (class evidence).
No analytics are added for the pilot; everything else is observed or asked.

### Does it work in a classroom? (usability and reliability)

| Measure | How | Good enough to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Learners who reach their first sound without adult help | Observer tally | 9 in 10 |
| Learners who join the class with their code on the first or second try | Observer tally | 9 in 10 |
| Time from "open Carmen" to the whole class playing | Stopwatch | under 5 minutes by the third session |
| Tries marked "input problem" out of all tries, by input kind | Class evidence (`unclear`) | under 1 in 10 on screen; record microphone separately |
| Tries that reached the teacher out of tries made | Compare device count with board count for 5 learners | all of them within a day |
| Sessions stopped by a technical problem | Teacher log | none after the first session |
| Device and browser mix actually used | UAT records | recorded, no target |

### Do teachers get something they can act on?

| Measure | How | Good enough to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Teacher can say, after a session, who to help next and why | Short interview, compare with the board | most teachers, most sessions |
| Times a teacher changed a next activity, a support or a skill judgement | Count of plan versions | recorded, no target; zero means the review is not useful |
| Times the teacher disagreed with a status on the board | Teacher log with the reason | every disagreement reviewed with the education owner |
| Minutes to set up a class of 25 | Stopwatch | under 15 |

### Do learners understand the feedback?

| Measure | How | Good enough to continue |
|---|---|---|
| Learner can say in their own words what the feedback asked them to try | Ask 5 learners per session | 4 in 5 |
| Learners who try again after feedback, out of those who did not pass | Class evidence (next attempt on the same activity) | recorded, no target |
| Feedback sentences a teacher or learner found wrong or confusing | Teacher log, exact sentence and lesson | every one fixed or explained |
| Learners who open "More help" | Observer tally (not logged by the app) | recorded, no target |

### Access

| Measure | How |
|---|---|
| Learners who needed bigger controls, slower, counting or hear-first, and whether it was offered in time | Teacher log and class evidence (`support`) |
| Learners who could not take part, and why | Teacher log |
| Screen reader, switch or keyboard-only use | Only if a participating learner uses one; record what blocked them |

### Not measured, on purpose

Musical ability, progress against the curriculum, comparisons between learners or classes, time on
task, streaks or engagement. A pilot this small cannot support them, and several are things Carmen
does not do.

## Before a pilot can start

1. A primary music educator has reviewed the activities to be used (docs/CURRICULUM_MATRIX.md).
2. The release checklist passes on the school's actual devices.
3. A hosted service with the owners in docs/OPERATIONS.md named.
4. The school's approval, privacy notice and consent process for the data in docs/CLASSROOM.md.
5. Ethics approval if results will be published or shared beyond the school and the team.

## Questions for the sponsor

Decisions the team cannot make alone. Each blocks something specific.

| # | Question | Blocks |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Who is the reviewing primary music educator, and how many hours do they have? | Any status above Draft; pilot content |
| 2 | Which school or schools, which year levels, and which devices do they actually have? | Device testing targets; pilot plan |
| 3 | Who is the data controller for class evidence: the school, the sponsor or the team? Who signs the agreement with the school? | Switching classes on for real learners |
| 4 | Which privacy rules apply (state department policy, the Privacy Act, the school's own)? Is an approved-software assessment needed (for example a state department's risk review)? | Hosting choice; timeline |
| 5 | Where must data be hosted (Australia only?) and who pays for and administers the hosting account? | Creating the hosted project |
| 6 | Is syncing learner evidence to teachers wanted at all for the pilot, or should the pilot run device-only first? | Scope of the first pilot |
| 7 | How long should class evidence be kept? (Set to 400 days; easy to change.) | Retention schedule |
| 8 | Do teachers sign in with their department email, and can a one-time code email reach those inboxes? Is single sign-on required later? | Sign-in design |
| 9 | Is the microphone allowed on school devices, and are headphones available? | Whether Use my instrument is in the pilot |
| 10 | Who names the owners in docs/OPERATIONS.md, and who supports teachers during the pilot? | Any pilot |
| 11 | Will results be published or presented? If so, which ethics process applies? | Consent wording; what may be recorded |
| 12 | What is the sponsor's success test for the project at the showcase: a working class demo, a pilot plan, or pilot results? | Priorities for the remaining time |
| 13 | May the team contact music education researchers or associations for content review, and under whose name? | Finding a reviewer |
| 14 | Is there a budget for licensed assets or content if the reviewer asks for changes the CC0 samples cannot meet? | Content scope |
