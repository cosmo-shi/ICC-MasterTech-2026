## 2. Further problems found in this audit

- **Advice that is wrong for some rhythms.** `listenCoach.ts` copy tip for "uneven" says "make every space the same". `rhythmFeedback` says "Keeping the spaces between taps even helps" for early taps, and "Even spaces between the taps keep the rhythm steady" for a matched take. Both are wrong for rhythms with quavers, minims or rests.
- **Free play is judged against a guessed beat.** `coachFree` presents its beat (from the first three gaps) as fact, and its "uneven" tip assumes the learner wanted a steady beat.
- **Two coaches disagree.** A lesson runs both `decide()` and `coachCopy()`, which can pick different focuses for the same take.
- **The feedback sheet is not a real dialog.** It has `role="dialog"` with `aria-modal="false"`, no focus management, no Escape, and the page behind stays active. It cannot be reopened after it closes, and a support choice is applied without saying so.
- **Overlapping playback.** The coach reply, "Hear the difference" and "Hear it" use separate players, so two can sound at once.
- **CSS has grown in layers.** `src/index.css` is 5,623 lines with 50 media queries. 56 selectors are defined more than once at top level, for example `.chip` 3 times and `.btn` twice. There are four dated patch layers, and fixed heights and `max-height` rules work against each other.
- **Recording and listening states are uneven.** Permission denied, no device and MIDI unsupported have messages. An interrupted recording (page hidden), a disconnected MIDI device mid-activity, a storage-quota failure and "analysis unavailable" have none, or only a generic one.
