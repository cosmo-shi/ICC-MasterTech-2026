- **Years 1-2.** Responding: By the end of Year 2, students identify where they experience music. … (full text in source) Making: Students demonstrate listening skills. … use the elements of music to improvise and/or compose music, and sing and play music in informal settings (paraphrased from source).
- **Years 3-4.** Responding: By the end of Year 4, students describe the use of elements of music in music they compose, perform and/or experience. … (full text in source) Making: Students demonstrate listening skills when performing and composing. … (full text in source)
- **Years 5-6.** Responding: By the end of Year 6, students explain how elements of music are manipulated in music they compose, perform and/or experience. … (full text in source) Making: Students demonstrate listening and aural skills when composing and performing. … notate, document and/or record the music they compose … (full text in source)

## Known gaps

- Exploring and responding (E01, E02) has no Carmen activity in any band: no listening to music from other cultures, times and places yet.
- E02 (First Nations Australians) needs authorised material and cultural advice; Carmen will not invent or imitate it.
- Presenting and performing is only informal playing on the device; sharing with an audience is optional and outside the app.
- Singing is not covered (keyboard and drums only).
- Foundation descriptors are Arts-wide; Carmen covers only the music part of D01 and C01.
- No mapping is checked by a primary music educator yet.
